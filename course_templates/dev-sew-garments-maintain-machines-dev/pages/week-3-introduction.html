Week 3: Introduction

 

 

Topic overview

 

This week focuses on specialist sewing machines used in knit garment production, safe operating procedures and responding to machine faults. You will build the technical knowledge required to safely set up and operate equipment before beginning your first complete knit garment, the Track Pant, which forms the next stage of your practical assessment work.

 

 

This week you will

 

  • Explore specialist sewing machines used for knit garment construction.
  • Practise safe operating procedures for industrial sewing equipment.
  • Identify common machine faults and apply corrective actions.
  • Complete practical activities supporting the Knit Sewing Workbook assessment.
  • Review Track Pant construction requirements and assembly processes.

 

 

 

Learning Objectives

 

  • Demonstrate safe operating procedures when using industrial sewing machines in a production room environment.
  • Identify specialist sewing machines and select appropriate equipment for specific knit garment operations.
  • Apply machine setup requirements, including threading, lubrication and operational checks before sewing.
  • Analyse common sewing faults and determine suitable corrective actions to maintain production quality.
  • Evaluate stitch quality and machine performance against workplace and garment production requirements.
  • Implement assembly planning and machine selection processes to prepare for Track Pant construction.
