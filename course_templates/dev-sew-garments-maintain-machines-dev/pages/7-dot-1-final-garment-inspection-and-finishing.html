Final Garment Inspection & Finishing

Before submitting your Hooded Sweat Top, carry out a final quality inspection. In industry, every garment is checked before it leaves the production line to make sure it meets workplace quality standards.

Taking a few extra minutes to inspect, finish and press your garment can make a real difference to the overall quality of your work.

 

Final Garment Inspection

Final garment inspection checklist

Check

What to look for
Stitch quality Stitches are even, secure and free from skipped stitches or loose loops.
Seam quality Seams are straight, consistent and lie flat without puckering.
Hood construction The hood is symmetrical and attached evenly to the neckline.
Sleeves & cuffs Sleeves are inserted correctly and cuffs are even without twisting.
Hem band The hem band is attached evenly with no twisting or stretching.
Labels Brand and care labels are correctly positioned and securely attached.
Drawcord The drawcord is threaded correctly and moves freely through the hood.
Overall appearance The garment is clean, pressed and ready for presentation.

Finishing Techniques

  • Trim all loose threads from the garment.
  • Press seams as required to improve the garment's appearance.
  • Check that labels are securely attached.
  • Ensure the drawcord has been inserted correctly.
  • Remove any chalk marks or temporary markings.
  • Inspect the garment from the front, back and inside before submission.

 

Common Final Inspection Faults

Common final inspection faults and how to correct them

Fault

How to correct it
Loose threads Trim carefully using thread snips.
Puckered seam Press the seam, or restitch if required.
Twisted cuff or hem band Remove and reattach correctly if necessary.
Uneven topstitching Restitch if it affects the garment quality.
Missed overlocking Repair before submitting the garment.
Creased garment Carry out a final press following workplace procedures.

 

Leaving Your Workstation

Before leaving the production room, make sure your workstation is ready for the next operator.

  • Turn off your sewing machine.
  • Remove lint and thread waste from the machine and work area.
  • Return all tools and equipment to their correct storage location.
  • Replace the machine cover if one is provided.
  • Report any machine faults or maintenance requirements to your teacher.
