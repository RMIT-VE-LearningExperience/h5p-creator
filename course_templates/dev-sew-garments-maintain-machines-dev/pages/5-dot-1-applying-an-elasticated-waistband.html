Applying an Elasticated Waistband

 

Image by  kolotype on Adobe Stock Licenced under Education License 

Elasticated waistbands are commonly used in stretch knit garments because they provide comfort, flexibility and ease of movement.

When applying elastic to the Track Pant, it is important that the elastic is joined securely, distributed evenly and enclosed neatly inside the waistband.

Expand each section below to review the construction steps, best practice and common faults.

 

Applying an Elasticated Waistband

Elasticated Waistband Construction

Step 1 – Prepare the elastic

Check the elastic length against the assessment instructions. Make sure the elastic is not twisted, damaged or stretched out before sewing.

 

Image source: The Sewing Directory

 

 

Step 2 – Join the elastic

Overlap the elastic ends as instructed and stitch securely. The join must be flat and strong enough to withstand stretch during wear.

 

Image source: The Creative Curator

Image source: The Sewing Directory

 

 

Step 3 – Prepare the waistband

Join the waistband seam and press if required. Check that the waistband is the correct size and that seams are aligned before inserting the elastic.

 

Image source: The Sewing Directory

 

 

Step 4 – Insert the elastic

Place the elastic inside the waistband carefully. Make sure the elastic is flat and not twisted inside the casing.

 

Image source: The Sewing Directory

 

 

Step 5 – Secure the elastic

Stitch at key points as instructed to help prevent the elastic from rolling or twisting inside the waistband.

 

 

 

Step 6 – Attach the waistband to the pant

Match notches and seams before attaching the waistband to the pant waist. Distribute fullness evenly as you sew.

 

 

 

Best Practice

Best practice techniques and why they matter

Technique

Why it matters
Check for twists Elastic must sit flat inside the waistband. Twisted elastic can feel uncomfortable and may affect the garment fit.
Match seams and notches Matching notches and seams helps distribute the waistband evenly around the pant waist.
Distribute fullness evenly Uneven distribution can make the waistband look gathered in some areas and stretched in others.
Do not catch the elastic When stay stitching or securing the waistband, take care not to accidentally stitch through the elastic unless instructed.
Check before moving on Once the waistband is attached, check that it is even, secure and correctly aligned before continuing to the next step.

 

Common Faults When Applying Elastic

Common faults, causes and corrections

Fault

Possible cause

Correction or prevention
Elastic is twisted Elastic was not checked before stitching the waistband closed. Check the elastic is flat before stitching. Stop and correct twists before continuing.
Uneven waistband gathers Waistband and pant waist were not evenly matched. Match notches and distribute fullness evenly before sewing.
Elastic join is bulky Elastic was overlapped unevenly or stitched too heavily. Overlap accurately and stitch securely without creating excess bulk.
Elastic rolls inside waistband Elastic was not secured correctly or the casing is too loose. Follow the assembly plan and secure the elastic where instructed.
Waistband seams do not align Seams and notches were not matched before sewing. Check seam alignment before stitching. Unpick and correct if required.
