Week 2: Introduction

 

 

Topic overview

 

This week focuses on overlock machines, needle selection, machine maintenance, troubleshooting and garment quality standards used in stretch knit garment production. These skills are essential for operating industrial sewing equipment safely and producing quality outcomes in apparel manufacturing workplaces. The knowledge developed this week prepares you for workbook activities and supports upcoming track pant and hoodie construction tasks.

 

 

This week you will

 

  • Explore 3, 4 and 5 thread overlock machines and applications.
  • Select suitable needles for different stretch knit fabric types.
  • Practise cleaning, maintaining and checking industrial sewing machines safely.
  • Identify common sewing faults and apply basic troubleshooting techniques.
  • Inspect samples against quality standards and record corrective actions.

 

 

 

Learning Objectives

 

  • Apply appropriate needle selection methods for stretch knit fabrics and sewing operations.
  • Demonstrate safe setup, cleaning and maintenance procedures for industrial sewing machines.
  • Analyse common sewing faults and identify likely causes affecting stitch quality.
  • Implement machine adjustments to improve sewing performance and product quality.
  • Evaluate sewn samples against workplace quality standards and determine corrective actions.
  • Demonstrate correct machine selection for knit garment construction and edge finishing requirements.
