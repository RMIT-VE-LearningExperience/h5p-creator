Instructions

  1. Watch the video explaining knit fabric structures, stretch behaviour and common knit fabric types.
  2. Consider how fabric stretch, recovery and handling characteristics influence sewing methods, garment quality and construction efficiency.
  3. Identify two key differences or insights to post in the discussion forum before class.

 

Purpose: This activity prepares you to recognise the behaviour of plain jersey, rib knit and French terry fabrics before working with them in class. It also develops the observation skills needed when selecting sewing methods, following assembly plans and completing quality checks during garment construction. 

 

 

Preparation Before Class 

 

Before class checklist:

  • Wear closed-toe shoes
  • Tie back long hair
  • Bring your equipment and student login details
  • Review the Track Pant assessment task
  • Bring any questions about the assembly plan or quality checklist
  • Be ready to work safely and independently in the production room

 

Be ready to:

  • Prepare your work area, fabric pieces and machine
  • Follow WHS and safe work procedures
  • Use the correct machines for the task
  • Control fabric and machine speed while sewing
  • Inspect work against quality standards
  • Submit clear photo evidence of your completed work
