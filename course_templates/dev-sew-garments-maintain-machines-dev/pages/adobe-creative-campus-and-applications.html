Adobe Creative Campus and applications

 

 

RMIT is an Adobe Creative Campus

 

RMIT University has reiterated our commitment to driving digital innovation, transforming creative technology, and fostering access and equity to technological transformations through our Adobe Creative Campus partnership.

All programs that use specific Adobe tools as part of the student learning and assessment process have access to the entire Adobe Creative Cloud suite. 

All RMIT students have access to Adobe Express Premium.

Adobe Express is an all-in-one design, photo and video tool that brings the best from the Adobe Creative Suite into one place, empowering you to produce unique and beautiful content quickly and intuitively. It’s a fantastic tool to use for assessment tasks, providing access to stock, webpage and animation tools, and much more.

You can access Adobe Express (Premium) for free. Simply log in to the user-friendly platform through your web browser or the Adobe Express mobile app with your RMIT credentials to access a range of tools and resources to bring your creative vision to life.
