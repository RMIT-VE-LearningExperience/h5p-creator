Wominjeka!

Welcome! In this course, you will develop the skills and knowledge required to sew stretch knit garments using industrial sewing machines and equipment. You will also learn how to set up, adjust and maintain industrial sewing machines so they remain in safe working condition. Throughout the course, you will continue to build your understanding of industry terminology, safe work practices, standard operating procedures and quality expectations.
