Week 1: Introduction

 

 

Topic overview

 

This week introduces the production room environment, workplace safety requirements and the industrial sewing machines used throughout the course. You will learn how to set up a safe workstation, identify key machine types and begin developing lockstitch machine operation skills that form the foundation for future knit garment construction and machine maintenance activities.

 

 

This week you will

 

  • Explore the production room, safety procedures and workplace expectations.
  • Set up and organise a safe and efficient sewing workstation.
  • Identify industrial sewing machines and their production applications.
  • Examine lockstitch machine components, threading pathways and operation.
  • Practise basic machine preparation, oil checking and sewing control skills.

 

 

 

Learning Objectives

 

  • Apply workplace health and safety requirements when working in a clothing production environment.
  • Demonstrate correct workstation setup and housekeeping practices for safe machine operation.
  • Identify industrial sewing machines and select suitable equipment for specific sewing tasks.
  • Demonstrate recognition of key lockstitch machine components and their functions during operation.
  • Apply basic machine preparation procedures including threading, lubrication checks and safety inspections.
  • Analyse common causes of sewing faults and implement appropriate preventative actions during production.
