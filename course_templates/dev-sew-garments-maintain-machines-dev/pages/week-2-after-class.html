Week 2: After Class

 

 

After Class: Understanding Overlock Machines, Needle Selection, Machine Maintenance and Quality

 

Format: Discussion

After our class on overlock machines, needle selection, machine maintenance, troubleshooting and quality standards, complete the following self-check activity to reinforce your understanding and prepare for upcoming assessment tasks. This checklist prepares you for AT1 Knit Sewing Workbook, AT2 Make 2 Knit Products – Track Pant and AT4 Knowledge Quiz and Workbook.

 

Instructions

  1. Review the concepts and practical demonstrations covered in class.
  2. Confirm your understanding using the Assessment Preparation Checklist below.
  3. You will demonstrate these skills and knowledge in AT1 Knit Sewing Workbook, AT2 Make 2 Knit Products – Track Pant and AT4 Knowledge Quiz and Workbook. 

 

Assessment Preparation Checklist

☑ I can identify when a ballpoint needle or stretch needle should be selected for sewing stretch knit fabrics and explain the consequences of using the wrong needle. (AT1 Knit Sewing Workbook, Criterion 1.4; AT4 Knowledge Quiz and Workbook, Section 3 Q3 & Q4)

☑ I can identify the main purpose and application of 3 thread, 4 thread and 5 thread overlock machines. (AT1 Knit Sewing Workbook, Criterion 2.1; AT4 Knowledge Quiz and Workbook, Section 3 Q1)

☑ I can match knit garment operations to the appropriate industrial sewing machine and stitch type. (AT1 Knit Sewing Workbook, Criterion 2.1; AT4 Knowledge Quiz and Workbook, Section 3 Q1)

☑ I can prepare a workstation safely by following PPE requirements, checking machine setup and completing a test stitch before production. (AT1 Knit Sewing Workbook, Criterion 4.1 & 4.2; AT4 Knowledge Quiz and Workbook, Section 1 Q1 & Q2)

☑ I can describe the steps used to check lubrication levels and identify when a machine should be reported for maintenance. (AT1 Knit Sewing Workbook, Criterion 4.4; AT2 Track Pant, Part B Criterion 4.4; AT4 Knowledge Quiz and Workbook, Section 1 Q1)

☑ I can explain how to clean an overlocker safely and identify tools used during the cleaning process. (AT1 Knit Sewing Workbook, Criterion 4.2; AT2 Track Pant, Part B Criterion 4.2)

☑ I can identify common sewing faults such as skipped stitches, thread breakage, puckering and uneven stitching and determine likely causes. (AT1 Knit Sewing Workbook, Criterion 6.1 & 6.2; AT4 Knowledge Quiz and Workbook, Section 3 Q7)

☑ I can test a sewn sample, identify required machine adjustments and record corrective actions. (AT1 Knit Sewing Workbook, Criterion 5.1, 5.2, 7.1 & 7.2; AT2 Track Pant, Part B Criterion 5.1 & 5.2)

☑ I can inspect sewing work against quality standards and decide when corrective action is required before production continues. (AT1 Knit Sewing Workbook, Criterion 2.3 & 3.1; AT2 Track Pant, Part A Criterion 3.1; AT4 Knowledge Quiz and Workbook, Section 1 Q4)

 

Purpose: Completing this checklist consolidates your understanding of machine selection, needle selection, maintenance procedures, troubleshooting and quality inspection practices. These skills directly support successful performance in AT1 Knit Sewing Workbook, AT2 Make 2 Knit Products – Track Pant and AT4 Knowledge Quiz and Workbook.
