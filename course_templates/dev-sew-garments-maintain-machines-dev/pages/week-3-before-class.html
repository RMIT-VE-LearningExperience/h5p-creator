Instructions

  1. Watch the video explaining specialist sewing machines used for knit garment production and the purpose of different stitch types.
  2. Consider how machine selection and machine setup influence garment quality, stretch, durability and production efficiency.
  3. Identify two key differences or insights to post in the discussion forum before class.

 

Purpose: This activity prepares you to recognise the specialist machines used during Track Pant construction and understand why different machines are selected for specific sewing operations. The knowledge will support later practical work involving machine setup, troubleshooting and knit garment construction.

 

 

Preparation Before Class 

 

Make sure you have:

  • Checked that your practical samples are up to date.
  • Reviewed the Week 2 Canvas pages if you need clarification.
  • Taken photos of completed samples if instructed by your teacher.
  • Written down any questions about machine operation or troubleshooting.
  • Read the Week 3 overview so you are prepared for specialist knit machines and the Track Pant assessment briefing.

 

Before class checklist:

  • Wear closed-toe shoes
  • Bring your equipment and student login details
  • Check your Assessment 1 workbook is up to date
  • Bring any questions about machine samples or workbook evidence
  • Be ready to review the Track Pant assessment task
