Week 1: After Class

 

 

After Class: Understanding Production Room Safety & Industrial Sewing Machines 

 

Format: Discussion

After our class on production room safety, workstation setup, industrial sewing machines and lockstitch machine operation, complete the following self-check activity to reinforce your understanding and prepare for upcoming assessment tasks. This checklist prepares you for AT1 Knit Sewing Workbook and AT4 Knowledge Quiz and Workbook.

 

Instructions

  1. Review the concepts and practical demonstrations covered in class.
  2. Complete the Week 1: Production Room Safety Quiz. 
  3. Confirm your understanding using the Assessment Preparation Checklist below.
  4. You will demonstrate these skills and knowledge in AT1 Knit Sewing Workbook and AT4 Knowledge Quiz and Workbook. 

 

Assessment Preparation Checklist

☑ I can identify the main production room safety requirements, including keeping work areas tidy, maintaining clear walkways and following safety signage. (AT1 - Marking Criteria 4.1; AT4 - Section 1, Q1)

☑ I can identify appropriate PPE requirements, including enclosed footwear, tied-back hair and machine-specific protective equipment where required. (AT1 - Marking Criteria 4.1; AT4 - Section 1, Q2)

☑ I can explain why hazards such as loose clothing, cluttered walkways and exposed feet create risks in a production room environment. (AT1 - Marking Criteria 4.1; AT4 - Section 1, Q1)

☑ I can identify the primary purpose of a lockstitch machine and describe common operations completed using a 301 lockstitch. (AT1 - Marking Criteria 1.1; AT4 - Section 2, Q3)

☑ I can match common sewing tasks to the correct industrial machine, including lockstitch machines, overlockers and coverstitch machines. (AT1 - Marking Criteria 1.1; AT4 - Section 3, Q1)

☑ I can identify key lockstitch machine parts, including the tension disc, take-up lever, thread guides and needle. (AT1 - Marking Criteria 4.2; Assessed throughout AT4)

☑ I can explain the purpose of checking machine threading, needle condition and lubrication before operating a sewing machine. (AT1 - Marking Criteria 4.4; AT4 - Section 3, Q7)

☑ I can describe how a lockstitch is formed using a needle thread and bobbin thread. (AT1 - Marking Criteria 5.1; Assessed throughout AT4)

☑ I can identify common causes of sewing faults and explain basic corrective actions before commencing production work. (AT1 - Marking Criteria 6.1 and 6.2; AT4 - Section 3, Q7)

☑ I can explain how correct machine setup and safe operating practices contribute to garment quality and efficient production. (AT1 - Marking Criteria 1.4 and 5.2; AT4 - Section 1, Q4)

 

Purpose: Completing this checklist consolidates your understanding of production room safety, machine selection, lockstitch machine operation and basic machine maintenance requirements. These skills and knowledge directly support successful completion of AT1 Knit Sewing Workbook and AT4 Knowledge Quiz and Workbook.
