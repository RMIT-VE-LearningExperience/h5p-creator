Succeed VE: digital literacy

 

 

Support for digital learning

 

We have designed a course to give you access to useful student resources and learning tools. You will find out how to use our Learning Management System (LMS) called Canvas. You will learn the best way to access information you need to complete your course, find out how to seek support and stay connected with your teacher and fellow students. 

You are automatically enrolled into this course and you can find it on your dashboard in Canvas.
