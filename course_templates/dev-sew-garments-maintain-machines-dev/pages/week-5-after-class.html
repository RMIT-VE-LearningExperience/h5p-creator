Week 5: After Class

 

 

After Class: Understanding Elasticated Waist Application, Overlocking and Pressing

 

Format: Discussion

After our class on elasticated waist application, overlocking seams and pressing during construction, complete the following self-check activity to reinforce your understanding and prepare for upcoming assessment tasks. This checklist prepares you for AT2 Make 2 Knit Products – Track Pant and AT3 Make Knit Products – Oversize Hoodie Sweat Top.

 

Instructions

  1. Review the concepts and practical demonstrations covered in class.
  2. Confirm your understanding using the Assessment Preparation Checklist below.
  3. You will demonstrate these skills and knowledge in AT2 Make 2 Knit Products – Track Pant and AT3 Make Knit Products – Oversize Hoodie Sweat Top. 

 

Assessment Preparation Checklist

☑ I can prepare, join and inspect waistband elastic so it is flat, secure and free from twists before insertion into a garment. (AT2 Part A, Criterion 2.1)

☑ I can insert elastic into a waistband and maintain correct alignment of seams and notches during construction. (AT2 Part A, Criterion 2.1)

☑ I can identify common elastic waistband faults such as twisted elastic, uneven gathers and misaligned seams, and explain how to correct them. (AT2 Part A, Criterion 3.2)

☑ I can overlock stretch knit seams while maintaining consistent seam allowance and correctly aligned fabric layers. (AT2 Part A, Criterion 2.1)

☑ I can inspect an overlocked seam for skipped stitches, unbalanced tension, puckering and incomplete seam capture. (AT2 Part A, Criterion 3.1; AT3 Part A, Criterion 3.1)

☑ I can identify the cause of common overlocking faults and determine an appropriate corrective action before continuing construction. (AT2 Part B, Criterion 6.1 & 6.2)

☑ I can demonstrate correct pressing techniques by pressing seams flat, reducing bulk and avoiding distortion of stretch knit fabrics. (AT2 Part A, Criterion 2.1)

☑ I can identify common pressing problems such as shiny fabric surfaces, stretched fabric and bulky seams, and explain how to prevent them. (AT2 Part A, Criterion 3.2; AT3 Part A, Criterion 3.2)

☑ I can evaluate a completed Track Pant for waistband quality, seam quality, pressing quality and overall presentation against assessment requirements. (AT2 Part A, Criterion 3.1 & 3.3)

 

Purpose: Completing this checklist consolidates the practical construction, quality inspection and fault identification skills covered during the week. These skills directly support successful completion of AT2 Make 2 Knit Products – Track Pant and establish quality standards that will continue into AT3 Make Knit Products – Oversize Hoodie Sweat Top.
