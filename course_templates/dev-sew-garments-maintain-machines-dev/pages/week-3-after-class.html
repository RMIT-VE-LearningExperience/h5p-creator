Week 3: After Class

 

 

After Class: Understanding Specialist Machines, SOPs and Track Pant Preparation

 

Format: Discussion

After our class on specialist knit machines, safe operating procedures, corrective actions and Track Pant preparation, complete the following self-check activity to reinforce your understanding and prepare for upcoming assessment tasks. This checklist prepares you for AT1 Knit Sewing Workbook, AT2 Make 2 Knit Products – Track Pant and AT4 Knowledge Quiz and Workbook.

 

Instructions

  1. Review the concepts and practical demonstrations covered in class.
  2. Confirm your understanding using the Assessment Preparation Checklist below.
  3. You will demonstrate these skills and knowledge in AT1 Knit Sewing Workbook, AT2 Make 2 Knit Products – Track Pant and AT4 Knowledge Quiz and Workbook.

 

Assessment Preparation Checklist

☑ I can identify the purpose of a coverstitch machine and explain where it is commonly used on knit garments. (AT1 Knit Sewing Workbook, Sample 5 and Sample 6; AT4 Section 3, Q1)

☑ I can identify the purpose of a mock flatlock machine and describe a suitable knit garment application for this stitch type. (AT1 Knit Sewing Workbook, Sample 4; AT4 Section 3, Q1)

☑ I can explain why specialist machines are selected according to the fabric, garment and sewing operation being completed. (AT4 Section 3, Q1; Assessed throughout AT2 Part A)

☑ I can follow the key steps of a sewing machine SOP before starting work, including PPE, machine checks and test stitching. (AT1 Marking Criteria 4.1 and 4.4; AT2 Part B, 4.1 and 4.4; AT4 Section 1, Q1 and Q2)

☑ I can identify the correct corrective action for thread breaking, skipped stitches, unusual machine noise and fabric jams. (AT1 Marking Criteria 6.1 and 6.2; AT2 Part B, 6.1 and 6.2; AT4 Section 3, Q7)

☑ I can explain why machine faults must be reported and addressed before continuing production. (AT1 Marking Criteria 6.2 and 7.2; AT2 Part B, 6.2 and 7.2; AT4 Section 3, Q6)

☑ I can identify the machines used in Track Pant construction, including the overlocker, lockstitch machine and coverstitch machine. (AT2 Part A, Item 3 Identify the machines used in the construction of the Track Pant)

☑ I can explain the purpose of an assembly plan and how it helps sequence garment construction efficiently. (AT2 Part A, Item 1 Make an Adult Track Pant according to the job specifications assembly plan; AT4 Section 2, Q1)

☑ I can identify quality requirements during Track Pant production, including stitch quality, seam allowance accuracy, stretch performance, matching notches, thread trimming and pressing. (AT2 Part A, 3.1 and 3.2)

☑ I can describe a workplace hazard related to Track Pant construction and explain an appropriate control measure to reduce the risk. (AT2 Part A Observational Safety Checklist; AT4 Section 1, Q1 and Q2)

 

Purpose: Completing this checklist consolidates your understanding of specialist machine selection, safe operating procedures, fault identification and Track Pant preparation requirements. These skills directly support successful completion of AT1 Knit Sewing Workbook, AT2 Make 2 Knit Products – Track Pant and AT4 Knowledge Quiz and Workbook.
