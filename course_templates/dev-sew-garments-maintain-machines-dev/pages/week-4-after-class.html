Week 4: After Class

 

 

After Class: Understanding Stretch Knit Fabrics, Production Documents and Quality Checking

 

Format: Discussion

After our class on stretch knit fabric characteristics, knit fabric identification, assembly plans, spec packs and quality checklists, complete the following self-check activity to reinforce your understanding and prepare for upcoming assessment tasks. This checklist prepares you for AT2 Make 2 Knit Products – Track Pant, AT3 Make Knit Products – Oversize Hoodie Sweat Top and AT4 Knowledge Quiz and Workbook.

 

Instructions

  1. Review the concepts and practical demonstrations covered in class.
  2. Confirm your understanding using the Assessment Preparation Checklist below.
  3. You will demonstrate these skills and knowledge in AT2 Make 2 Knit Products – Track Pant, AT3 Make Knit Products – Oversize Hoodie Sweat Top and AT4 Knowledge Quiz and Workbook. 

 

Assessment Preparation Checklist

☑ I can identify the key characteristics of stretch knit fabrics and explain how stretch, recovery and fabric behaviour influence garment construction decisions. (AT4 Section 1, Q5)

☑ I can distinguish between plain jersey, rib knit and French terry fabrics and select an appropriate use for each fabric type. (AT4 Section 1, Q5)

☑ I can identify handling practices that prevent wavy seams, twisted seams and fabric distortion when sewing stretch knit fabrics. (AT2 Part A, Criterion 2.2)

☑ I can explain the purpose of a specification pack and locate construction information needed to manufacture a garment consistently. (AT2 Part A, Criterion 3.1)

☑ I can interpret an assembly plan and identify the correct sequence of garment construction stages from preparation through to finishing. (AT4 Section 2, Q1)

☑ I can identify machine abbreviations such as PL, O/L, C/S and M/S and select the correct machine for a construction operation. (AT2 Part A, Criterion 1.1)

☑ I can explain why garment operations must be completed in the correct sequence and describe problems that may occur if the sequence is not followed. (AT4 Section 2, Q1)

☑ I can use a quality checklist to inspect seam quality, stitch type, measurements and garment alignment during construction. (AT2 Part A, Criterion 3.1)

☑ I can identify faults during construction and record corrective or preventative actions to maintain garment quality standards. (AT2 Part A, Criterion 3.2)

 

Purpose: Completing this checklist consolidates your understanding of fabric behaviour, production documentation, construction sequencing and quality control processes used in stretch knit garment manufacturing. These skills directly support successful completion of AT2 Make 2 Knit Products – Track Pant, AT3 Make Knit Products – Oversize Hoodie Sweat Top and AT4 Knowledge Quiz and Workbook.
